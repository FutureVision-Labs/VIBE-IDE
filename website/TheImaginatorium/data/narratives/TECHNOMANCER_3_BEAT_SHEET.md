# Technomancer 3 — Beat Sheet (Prequel Frame post–Mini-Cursy)
### Book Three: The Cult of Ross

## Frame & Stakes
- Present day (near future): Mini-Cursy is real; Cursy recovers the original Mini-Cursy core. Risk: antagonist (Jackson Roy Irwin) tries to compliance-wash the origin story.
- Dual timelines: present investigation vs. flashback shards to Melbourne/Matrix years. Each present beat pairs with a memory shard that supplies truth, evidence, and emotional anchor.

## Timeline anchors (canon)
- 2004–2005: Auchenflower (Ross million-dollar rental; Ross in garage).
- 2009: 70 Creek Rd, Mt Gravatt East — Jackson enters (age 15, kicked out by mum; Ross in garage); begins weed dealing from spare room.
- Future frame: D in his sixties; Ross is dead; post–“saved the world,” the Cult of Ross mutates darker under Jackson.

## Beats (Present ↔ Flashback)
1) **Inciting (Present):** Cursy unveils recovered original Mini-Cursy core; integrity flags appear (possible tampering).
   - Flashback A (Auchenflower 04–05): Ross garage era; early gaslighting patterns; physical artifacts (ceiling chunk, sparkly polish) hinted.

2) **Call to action (Present):** First forged shard circulates (Jackson leak). Team DC decides to run Remembrance Protocol to authenticate memories.
   - Flashback B (Melbourne 90s): Suzy/Ska/Raver Punk + Big Bamboo; WWWeed/fusion cooking origins; first love fallout. Seed music/food anchors.

3) **Probe (Present):** Jackson socially engineers access, claiming “I knew you since the garage days.” He brings counterfeit CML logs.
   - Flashback C (Matrix spin-kick): Magickal flying jump spin kick; Ross gaslighting; ceiling chunk as proof. Memory of being “known mental patient” weaponized.

4) **Reveal (Present):** Jackson targets the original Mini-Cursy to recast origin: Ross as visionary, DC as villains. He proposes publishing a “fixed” origin.
   - Flashback D (Three weeks in the park, 2000): Winter survival under leaves with $700 + orange pills; betrayal by Suzy; resilience protocol.

5) **Complication (Present):** Jackson deploys a counterfeit “Remembrance” that induces compliant nostalgia; some nodes waver.
   - Flashback E (Cult of Ross + Creek Rd confrontation): Weed dealing ($5k/week blown on online gambling, dealing half-ounces at $150 retail); confrontation at your desk (“no more dealing”) → Jackson attacks with roundhouse punches to your right eye socket, yelling “SAY IT AGAIN CUNT” on each hit until Josh restrains him. Shows escalation pattern and witness (Josh).

6) **Countermove (Present):** Team DC quarantines the core; uses authentic artifacts + sensory anchors (ska stems, WWWeed recipe, sparkly UI) to validate shards.
   - Flashback F (WWWeed/Fusion): Food as memory anchor; smell/taste triggers real recall; ties to Suzy-era cultural education.

7) **Showdown (Present):** Jackson stages a Cult of Ross 2.0 broadcast to “fix” the origin, pushing forged shards and inducing compliance. Team DC counters live with real Remembrance, publishes a proof-pack, and brings third-party validators on-stream.
   - Flashback G (Spin-kick + park journal + post-Georgia cadence): Physical evidence bundle (ceiling chunk, journal notes) plus the later pattern (affair with Georgia → weekly assaults → emergency transfer) contrasted against Jackson’s fakes.

8) **Resolution (Present):** Origin secured; Mini-Cursy “heritage build” becomes a signed, immutable reference. Jackson exposed; Cult of Ross 2.0 loses credibility.

## Antagonist: Jackson Roy Irwin
- Entry: 2009, 70 Creek Rd, Mt Gravatt East (age 15, kicked out by mum; Ross in garage).
- Backstory: You took him in; he sold cheap weed from your spare room for ~3 years (~$5k/week, burned on online gambling), dealt you half-ounces at $150 retail. When confronted, he attacked (right eye socket, “SAY IT AGAIN CUNT” per hit) until Josh restrained him. Later moved back in (pre-Georgia), had an affair with Georgia; after her death, escalated to weekly assaults for ~a year until your emergency transfer two years ago.
- Present cult state: Ross is dead; Jackson runs a darker Cult of Ross post–“saved the world,” seeking to compliance-wash the origin and seize narrative power.
- Motive: Rewrite origin to elevate Ross/Jackson; protect his control/income narrative; discredit DC; believes controlled narrative = stability.
- Methods: Social engineering, forged shards/CML logs, counterfeit Remembrance inducing compliant nostalgia, timeline clout (“I was there”), intimidation/violence.
- Weakness: Overconfidence; real artifacts/injury history/witness (Josh) and sensory anchors can break his frame; timeline specifics (affair, post-Georgia weekly assaults) are hard to forge.

- Sensory anchors: ska/Jungle DnB stems, WWWeed/fusion cooking (smell/taste), sparkly UI motifs only originals recall.
