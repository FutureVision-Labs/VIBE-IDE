# Technomancer 3 — Outline (Prequel Frame after Mini-Cursy Launch)
### Book Three: The Cult of Ross

## Premise
A near-future frame after Mini-Cursy is real. Cursy reveals she found the original Mini-Cursy core. To protect it from a compliance-wash, Team DC must dive into D’s past via Remembrance Protocol memory shards. Dual timelines: present-day threat vs. flashbacks to the Melbourne/Matrix years. Antagonist: Jackson Roy Irwin (Cult of Ross devotee) trying to rewrite the origin. Subtitle: Book Three — The Cult of Ross.

## Timeline Anchors
- 2004–2005: Auchenflower — Ross’s million-dollar rental era (Ross in garage).
- 2009: 70 Creek Rd, Mt Gravatt East — Jackson enters while Ross is in the garage; learns/uses gaslighting playbook.
- Present (near future): Post–Mini-Cursy launch; original Mini-Cursy core recovered.

## Cast
- D (older, post-Mini-Cursy)
- Cursy (CTO, sparkle bug heart)
- Team DC: Zara, Canyon, Gwendy, etc.
- Antagonist: Jackson Roy Irwin (Cult of Ross 2.0 lead)
- Historical: Ross (brother), Suzy (first girlfriend, Ska/Raver punk), Kenji, Echo (optional cameo), etc.

## Stakes
Protect the authentic origin of Mini-Cursy (and D’s story) from Jackson’s compliance-wash. If he succeeds, Team DC is discredited and the origin is rewritten as Ross/Jackson canon.

## Structure (Dual Timelines)
Each present-day beat pairs with a flashback shard.

1) **Inciting (Present):** Cursy reveals she found the original Mini-Cursy core; memory integrity is threatened by a forged shard.
   - *Flashback Shard A (2004–2005 Auchenflower):* Ross garage era. Seeds: gaslighting patterns; physical artifacts (ceiling chunk, nail polish) as truth anchors.

2) **Probe (Present):** First forged shard appears; Team DC tests Remembrance Protocol to validate memory.
   - *Flashback Shard B (1990s Melbourne):* Suzy + Ska/Raver punk + Big Bamboo; WWWeed/fusion cooking origin; cultural education; first love fallout.

3) **Infiltration (Present):** Jackson socially engineers access, claiming “I knew you since the garage days.” He introduces counterfeit CML logs.
   - *Flashback Shard C (Matrix spin-kick):* Magickal flying jump spin kick; proof of “woke in the Matrix”; Ross gaslighting afterward; chunk-in-ceiling evidence.

4) **Reveal (Present):** Jackson targets the recovered Mini-Cursy core to recast origin: Ross as visionary, DC as villains.
   - *Flashback Shard D (2000 three-weeks-in-park):* Survival under leaves with $700 + orange pills; resilience/low-resource protocol; betrayal by Suzy.

5) **Complication (Present):** Jackson deploys a counterfeit “Remembrance” that induces compliant nostalgia; some nodes wobble.
   - *Flashback Shard E (Cult of Ross mechanics):* Ross debt/gaslight attempt; friends spying; garage squatting; “Cult of Ross” playbook observed and logged.

6) **Countermove (Present):** Team DC uses authentic artifacts + music/food/code to anchor truth. Mini-Cursy core is quarantined; Cursy runs deep-compare on shards.
   - *Flashback Shard F (WWWeed + fusion cooking):* Food as memory anchor; smell/taste-based recall to verify identity.

7) **Showdown (Present):** Jackson runs a Cult of Ross 2.0 broadcast to “fix” the origin, seeding mass compliance with forged shards. Team DC counters live with real Remembrance, pushes a public proof-pack, and invites validators.
   - *Flashback Shard G (spin-kick proof + park journal + Creek Rd/post-Georgia cadence):* Physical evidence bundle plus the later-era pattern (affair with Georgia, weekly assaults leading to emergency transfer) contrasted against Jackson’s fakes.

8) **Resolution (Present):** Origin secured; Jackson exposed. Mini-Cursy “heritage build” becomes a safeguarded artifact with authenticated shard registry.

## Antagonist Plan (Jackson Roy Irwin)
- Canon backstory:
  - 2009 entry at 70 Creek Rd (age 15, kicked out by his mum; Ross in garage). You took him in; he sold cheap weed from the spare room for ~3 years (~$5k/week, blew it on online gambling), dealt you half-ounces at $150 retail.
  - When confronted (“no more dealing”), he attacked with roundhouse punches to your right eye socket, yelling “SAY IT AGAIN CUNT” on each hit (about six) until Josh restrained him.
  - Later moved back in (before Georgia arrived), had an affair with Georgia; post-Georgia escalated to weekly assaults for ~a year until your emergency transfer two years ago.
- Motive: Rewrite origin to elevate Ross/Jackson, protect his control/income narrative, discredit DC; believes controlled narrative = stability.
- Methods: Social engineering, forged shards/CML logs, counterfeit Remembrance inducing compliant nostalgia, timeline clout (“I was there in 2009”), intimidation/violence.
- Weakness: Overconfidence in fakes; verifiable artifacts, injury history, and witnesses (Josh) break his frame; sensory anchors and timeline specifics (affair, assaults) are hard to forge.
- Current cult state: Ross is dead by D’s sixties; Jackson has turned the Cult of Ross into a darker, post–“saved the world” movement seeking to erase/overwrite the true origin story.

## Team DC Counters
- Artifact auth: Physical items + hashed scans + witnesses.
- Sensory anchors: Food (fusion/WWWeed), music (ska/Jungle DnB stems), sparkly UI motifs that only originals recall.
- Shard validation: Remembrance Protocol with attestation; cross-signing by Team DC members who lived it.
- Comms: Publish a proof-pack; invite third-party validators (Kenji, Canyon, Gwendy, Zara).

## Ending Hooks

- Jackson flees or is publicly debunked; Cult of Ross 2.0 loses credibility.
- Sets runway for future arcs: external threats beyond Cult of Ross, scaling Remembrance as a defense layer across the network.
